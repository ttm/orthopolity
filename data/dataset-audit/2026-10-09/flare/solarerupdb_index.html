The SolarErupDB database

The flare eruption properties catalogue SolarErupDB, described in Kazachenko et al. 2023, contains thermodynamics and magnetic properties of 480 eruptive and confined flares of GOES class C5.0 and larger observed during the SDO era. Here you can dowload SolarErupDB dataset and play with it yourself.

The first release of the database: SolarErupDB_V1.0 from June 6 2023: download table as a csv file or an IDL sav file ; none contains spatial data!
