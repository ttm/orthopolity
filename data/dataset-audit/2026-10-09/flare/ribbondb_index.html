The RibbonDB database

The flare ribbon catalogue RibbonDB, described in Kazachenko et al. 2017, contains the active-region and flare-ribbon properties of all flares of GOES class C1.0 and larger observed during the SDO era, from April 2010 until April 2016, 3137 events total. Here you can dowload RibbonDB dataset and play with it yourself.

The first release of the database: RibbonDB_V1.0 from March 31 2017: download all files in zip

    Download by parts:

        1) RibbonDB_V1.0: IDL sav file or text CSV file

        2) Short description: readme_ribbondb_v1.0.txt

        3) Detailed description and analysis: Kazachenko et al. 2017, pdf
