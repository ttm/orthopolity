README: Synechococcus cellular P allocation 

This dataset contains measurements of cellular physiology, nutrient conditions, growth dynamics, and intracellular phosphorus (P) allocation in Synechococcus sp. WH8102 grown under controlled laboratory phosphorus treatments. Experiments were conducted in continuous cultures under three phosphorus conditions (Low-P, Mid-P, High-P), with multiple biological replicates and repeated sampling over time.

The dataset is now provided as a single integrated table, combining population-level measurements with intracellular phosphorus pool quantifications. Each row represents a single sample defined by treatment, day, and biological replicate (bottle).

Dataset structure

The table includes two main types of measurements:

Physiological and environmental variables
Intracellular phosphorus pool composition
Variables included
Experimental metadata
treatment: Phosphorus supply level (Low-P, Mid-P, High-P)
day: Sampling day of the experiment
bottle: Biological replicate identifier
Cell physiology and environmental conditions
cell_mL: Cell abundance (cells mL⁻¹)
FLUO_RFU: In vivo fluorescence (RFU), proxy for biomass
SRP_µM: Soluble reactive phosphorus concentration in the medium (µM)
biovolume_µm3: Average cellular biovolume (µm³)
growth_rates_1_day: Specific growth rate (d⁻¹), calculated from fluorescence changes and corrected for dilution
Intracellular phosphorus pools

Intracellular phosphorus is partitioned into major biochemical pools:

ATP (adenosine triphosphate)
DNA
RNA
P_lipid (phospholipid-associated phosphorus)
free_Pi (inorganic phosphate)
PolyP (polyphosphate storage)
TPP (total particulate phosphorus)

Each pool is reported in three complementary formats:

_uM: bulk concentration in the culture (µM)
_fmol_cell: per-cell quota (fmol cell⁻¹)
_fmol_um3: normalized to cell biovolume (fmol µm⁻³)

Notes

All measurements originate from steady-state continuous culture experiments.
Missing values indicate measurements not collected or lost for a given sample.
Units are consistent across all variables and are explicitly provided in column names.

Summary

Together, these measurements provide a comprehensive view of how phosphorus availability shapes cellular physiology, growth, and intracellular phosphorus allocation in Synechococcus sp. WH8102, linking environmental nutrient conditions to cellular biochemical composition and population dynamics.
