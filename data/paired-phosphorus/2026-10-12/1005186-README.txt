XRMA Single-Cell Stoichiometry Dataset README
Dataset Overview
This dataset contains single-cell elemental stoichiometry measurements for Synechococcus sp. WH8102 obtained using X-ray microanalysis (XRMA) combined with microscopy-based cell size analysis. Cells were grown under different phosphorus (P) availability conditions and sampled across multiple timepoints and biological replicates.Each row represents an individual cell and includes measurements of cell size, elemental quotas (carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus), derived concentrations, and stoichiometric ratios (C:N:P).Cell geometry is approximated using a rod-shaped model to estimate cell volume from projected area measurements.
Data Structure
*SampleID: Unique identifier for each single-cell measurement*Experimental sample label: Syn_Cell (Sequential index per sample; Cell identifier within sample)*exp: Experimental phosphorus treatment (Low P, Mid P, High P)*time (Sampling timepoint, days)*rep: Biological replicate identifier (e.g., a, b, c)*A_um2_xray: Cell projected area measured from XRMA-associated SEM imaging (µm2)*A_um2_imageJ: Cell projected area measured using ImageJ segmentation (µm2)*Volume_um3: Estimated cell volume assuming rod-shaped geometry, derived from area (µm3)*C_fg_cell: Carbon content per cell (fg C/ cell)*N_fg_cell: Nitrogen content per cell (fg N / cell)*P_fg_cell: Phosphorus content per cell (fg P/cell)*C_fg_um3: Carbon concentration normalized to volume (fgC / µm3)*N_fg_um3: Nitrogen concentration normalized to volume:(fg N / µm3)*P_fg_um3: Phosphorus concentration normalized to volume (fg P / µm3)*C_P: Carbon to phosphorus ratio; Stoichiometric ratio*C_N: Carbon to nitrogen ratio; Stoichiometric ratio*N_P: Nitrogen to phosphorus ratio; Stoichiometric ratioMethods Summary
* XRMA analysis: Single-cell elemental composition measured using X-ray microanalysis coupled with SEM imaging.* Cell area measurements: Two independent approaches were used:o XRMA-associated SEM imaging (A_um2_xray)o ImageJ-based image segmentation (A_um2_imageJ)* Cell volume estimation: Calculated using a rod-shaped geometric approximation based on projected area.* Stoichiometry calculations: Elemental quotas were used to compute C:N:P ratios at the single-cell level.
Missing Data* Missing values are not applicable (NA) or left blank where measurements were not obtained.Notes* All elemental quotas are reported at single-cell resolution.* Volume estimates assume a rod-shaped cell geometry.* Two independent size estimation methods are included for validation purposes
