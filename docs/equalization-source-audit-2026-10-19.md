# Equalization kinetics: source qualification and observation limits

19 October 2026. After the speech transfer, the next priority is a measured
exchange mechanism with an independently specified response to a constraint or
topology change. The [design note](equalization-release-design-2026-10-19.md)
derives a pair-to-chain forecast and an observation counterexample. **No new
empirical prediction is qualified or executed in this audit.** No experimental
trace, response workbook, video or figure image was downloaded or decoded.
Published numerical summaries and searchable article text were read and are
explicitly exposed; this is not a blind source search.

**20 October follow-up:** [public inventory checks](exchange-source-followup-2026-10-20.md)
and a [sharper observation test](exchange-observability-2026-10-20.md) refine this
audit. Initially empty acceptors can establish influx despite normalization;
the outstanding gate is quantitative kinetic transfer, not a claim that no
transport occurred.

Original metadata and methods are retained in
`data/physics-candidates/2026-10-19/relaxation/`, with exact URLs, byte sizes and
hashes in `acquisition.json`. Chemistry/compartment alternatives are included
because they implement the same physical exchange mechanism more directly.

## Physics: switching optical trap

Di Terlizzi et al. (2024), *Variance sum rule for entropy production*,
[Science DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh1823),
[arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.08565v2),
[Dryad archive](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.h44j0zpsw).

The public inventory identifies `SwitchingTrap.zip` (285,389,783 bytes;
publisher SHA-256 `e63a021d4b6cbf3e362d5ed06e4f7502fa7c8628741aabe1c77cdd402a11d541`).
It lists time, position and directly measured force traces at three switching
amplitudes. The instrument measures force through optical momentum balance.
The dataset metadata and rendered README are reachable; separate README
download attempts returned HTTP 401/403. The numerical zip was not requested.

**Decision:** this is a useful driven, single-coordinate experiment, but the
documented stationary switching protocol does not supply exchange of a common
budget among independently measured classes, or a designated drive-off cohort.
The two commanded trap positions are not two simultaneously measured resource
reservoirs. A known Langevin energy correction would be another conditional
physical realization, not the proposed allocation-release test. Stop this route
at conceptual qualification; do not reinterpret switching probabilities as
evidence for spontaneous equal resource allocation.

## Physics: non-equipartition archive

Li, Cao and Ng (2024), *Non-Hermitian non-equipartition theory for trapped
particles*, [article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10912716/),
[Figshare metadata](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/25196924).

The API lists a 103,384,665-byte data archive. The article develops analytical
Brownian dynamics and checks it with numerical simulations, including departures
under nonconservative forces. It is relevant theoretical context, but those
computed curves do not supply the missing new experimental exchange cohort.
The archive itself was not acquired. A Figshare landing-page fetch failed,
whereas its public metadata API succeeded; neither fact establishes raw-data
suitability. No empirical test is designated here.

## Closest physical mapping: droplet compartments

Bachler et al. (2021), *Permeation Studies across Symmetric and Asymmetric
Membranes in Microdroplet Arrays*,
[article](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.0c04939),
[retained XML source](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8014892/fullTextXML).

The study measures fluorescent permeants in droplet pairs and five-compartment
chains. Pair-calibrated permeability could in principle forecast a chain's
trajectory using independently measured volumes and contact areas. This closely
matches the exchange model under volume-weighted counting measure, neither
log-orthopolic nor linear-orthopolic size allocation.

**Observation obstacle:** each image's fluorescence is rescaled to keep the
summed signal constant, compensating for several possible loss processes.
Consequently the processed total cannot independently verify conservation.
Normalization does not force equal shares; common fractional losses cancel,
whereas unequal losses can mimic redistribution (proved in the design note).
That counterexample assumes positive initial stocks. Here the preparation
specifies fluorophore-free acceptors: reliable zero-to-positive observations
would rule out loss alone, while leaving conservation and rate transfer open.

The identified supplementary package supplies a PDF and process videos, not
an identified raw fluorescence/geometry time-series archive. The source fits
the chain response itself, which is not a transferred forecast. The higher-pH
pair/chain comparison also uses different pH values. **Not yet qualified:**
find raw stock/geometry measurements or independent observation/loss controls
before qualifying conservation and a quantitative pair-to-network forecast.
Do not declare the experiment invalid or the law rejected from this audit.

## Accessible alternative with an open boundary

Fernandes et al. (2023), *Droplet fluidics for time-dependent analysis of barrier
permeability in an epithelial barrier on chip system*,
[article](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3RA00470H),
[institutional dataset](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/477186/),
[DataCite identity](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.5258/SOTON/D2378).

The institutional page identifies the response workbook
`FITC_8_Channel_Device.xlsx`, a droplet-size workbook, an Origin project and
cell measurements. Only metadata and article XML were acquired. The experiment
samples an outlet under imposed flow; its static comparison is a different
assay, with a reported endpoint. Neither is the proposed closed-budget release
trajectory. An open-system transport study could be designed, but must include
effluent, chamber geometry, sampling and independent permeability. Outlet
concentration alone is not simultaneous resource allocation between the two
chambers. Keep this as a documented alternative, not a qualified neutral test.

## Exposure and next action

Search results and article text exposed published agreement/disagreement,
optical-trap entropy-production summaries, droplet pair/chain permeability
estimates, the approximately 30-minute five-droplet endpoint, and the chip's
reported peak and decay times. The nonlinear optical paper's analytical and
simulation conclusions are also known. Future designs must retain that history;
withholding numerical archive arrays would not erase publication exposure.

The immediate target is a **pair-to-network exchange prediction**, or a true
constraint-release experiment, with independent weights, kinetics and loss
controls. The five-node homogeneous chain predicts a slow relaxation time
$3+\sqrt5$ times that of the matched pair. This is a standard-model consequence
to test, not an observed result. Raw-data availability and measurement identity
must be resolved before freezing an estimator or fitting any target curve.
This audit changes the design priority, not the 43-run empirical/simulation
registry, the 612-test suite, or the already built article PDFs.
