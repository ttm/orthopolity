# Directions not justified by the present evidence

This records scope decisions, not permanent prohibitions. Revisit them if new arguments or
evidence change their basis. The [source audit](source-audit.md) and [critical assessment](criticism.md)
give the detailed reasoning.

**11 November 2026 scope update:** the author-approved
[revision roadmap](manuscript-roadmap-2026-11-10.md) explicitly develops the
proposed natural law and a [finite-domain cosmological conjecture](cosmological-extension.md).
These are active research directions. The limitations below concern claims
of established universality, unrestricted explanations or unsupported physical
identifications; they do not prohibit proposing and testing a specified law.

| Direction | Reason to set it aside |
|---|---|
| Announcing an established universal natural or cosmological law | Independently specified scope and sufficient empirical support for universality are missing. A deeper derivation is not required; testing a precise resource-symmetry postulate remains a valid research direction. |
| Claiming discovery of inverse-cost accounting | The identity and closely related ecological interpretations have substantial prior art. |
| Treating more deterministic power-law examples as confirmation | Response curves, wave identities, and packing capacities do not establish object-abundance distributions. |
| Explaining every departure with unrestricted “friction” | A fitted residual that can match every observation makes no independent prediction. |
| Inferring a generic exponent from the number of independent inputs | Random products do not obey that rule without additional conditions. |
| Deriving social or moral conclusions from the spectrum | Causation, policy counterfactuals, and normative judgments need separate evidence and arguments. |
| Explaining dark matter or dark energy themselves, or asserting a universal bits-to-energy conversion | No such physical model or quantitative prediction has been supplied. Interpreting the mass distribution of simulated dark-matter subhalos is a separate, now explicit cosmological question. |
| Promoting an ensemble principle from the current median | The estimands differ; mean-zero slopes need not give a flat mean spectrum. |
| Claiming an 8% temporal-variance bound | The compared studies sample different populations and do not partition one variance. |
| Re-mapping database exponents by their proximity to −1 or −2 | That selects a convention using the answer the test is meant to assess. |
| Guessing replacement size bounds for StudyID_07 | The raw metadata are inconsistent; correction needs source evidence, not a plausible-looking substitution. |
| Expanding the viewer or sonification as evidence for orthopolity | An inspection aid is not a physical or statistical validation. Its utility would need a separate study. |

The existing viewer and sonification code remain available. They are not part of the empirical
argument. Further software or interface expansion is optional and should follow a concrete need.

The mathematical reconstruction, source audit and reproducible diagnostics
support the continuing natural-law programme. A new conjecture must add a
specified physical claim and consequences; its status and tests remain explicit.
