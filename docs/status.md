**30 October — normal aggregation and natural allocation distinguished:** the
author's argument now appears concisely in main section 5, with the derivation
and examples in **supporting S3.9** and definitions in the [glossary](glossary.md).
Normality is a bell-shaped Gaussian form; additive aggregation gives it under
central-limit conditions. The **natural (orthopolic) distribution** names the
normalized neutral inverse-resource profile for independently fixed objects,
resource requirements, domain and reference measure. Homogeneous geometric
requirements give power-law densities with exponent **d+1 for log-orthopolic**
and **d for linear-orthopolic** allocation, both expressed relative to linear
size. The naming does not establish the prevalence of either family in nature.

The same ideal inverse-square candle flux gives intensity densities proportional
to **I^(-3/2)** under uniform-radius sampling and **I^(-5/2)** under uniform-volume
sampling. These sampled flux histograms are not object-allocation evidence.
Nested boxes retain exact complete-level accounting; mixed household inventories
remain an empirical question. Canonical harmonic modes show coexistence:
Gaussian coordinates and equal mean energy under mode counting, neither
log-orthopolic nor linear-orthopolic size allocation. The uniform-cut
fragmentation boundary in S3.8 remains explicit.

No empirical data, forecasts or registry entries were added. Six analytic
change-of-variable checks agree in normalization and mean within **8.88e-16**;
independent scientific review found no remaining blocker after precision edits.
All **692 tests** and registry verification pass, with **46 runs / 1,168
references**. Full `make all` was not rerun; the previously documented BioMe
byte-comparison limitation remains unresolved. The title and general-law
ambition are unchanged. Next develop independent allocation mechanisms or

## 3. Results to carry forward

**Definitions.** Log-orthopolic: equal resource per $d\ln k$; with resource $\propto k^d$, density
exponent $\alpha=d+1$. Linear-orthopolic: $\alpha=d$. Always state the measure.

**Mathematics** (known results, attributed in the documents):

- Vacuity: any power law is orthopolic for some resource ($q\propto k^{\alpha-1}$), so evidence
  comes only from resources identified independently.
- Proportional growth with entry and exit: $(\zeta-1)(\mu+\sigma^2\zeta/2)=\phi$, and in a
  stationary system $\phi=J_R/Q>0$, so $\zeta-1\approx1/(m\tau_{\rm res})$. Equal resource per log
  size is the long-residence limit (Reed 2001; Saichev, Malevergne and Sornette 2010).
- Catalogue rules: territory $\alpha=1+d_s/D_k$; transport $\alpha=1+w+z-d_F$; coordinate change
  $\alpha=1+\zeta/c$; martingale $\alpha=1+1/c$; proportional growth $\alpha\to2$; rate ratios.

## 4. Current assessment

The speech transfer adds an independent informational predictor that improves
on all four declared alternatives in a fixed family split. It makes the value
of specifying a resource before a target response concrete. The inferred
capacity account is established in the source literature; this comparison does
not distinguish its mechanism from orthopolity or establish neutral allocation.
Taken with chemical competition and the failed biological correction, it gives
the article useful predictive successes and informative limits. The next
scientific gain should distinguish mechanisms or explain a departure with new
inputs and unused outcomes, rather than accumulate another familiar exponent.

The chemistry study adds a substantial, real-data prediction gain from including
independently calibrated competition. It passes its declared finite-accuracy
criterion, while IAST performs slightly better and measurement-scale discrepancies
remain. This strengthens the framework's concrete account of constrained
allocation. It does not establish a new advantage beyond known adsorption
physics or settle the law's broad neutral-allocation claim.

The cantilever study now adds real-physics extrapolation with both conditional
successes and clear local-model failures. Its global damping/thermal mismatch
makes spatial constraints testable, while the modest aggregate improvement and
source qualifications prevent treating it as decisive general-law evidence.

The unified repository contains mathematical foundations, explicit mechanisms,
resource-transfer applications and negative or qualified empirical comparisons.
The [research brief](research-brief.md) explains the general-law programme;
[ongoing research](ongoing-research.md) connects its broader studies.

- Catalogue matches alone do not identify resources or independently establish
  a law; several listed exponents remain unverified.
- Aftershock and flare registered decision rules are inconclusive. Their primary
  intervals exclude both specified rival readings under the implemented analyses.
- Global lake ζ is practically equivalent to 2 under its declared iid margin,
  but Country uncertainty does not establish equivalence and the basin interval
  is absent from that original analysis. The literal script's positive verdict
  remains recorded. Its local-geometry follow-up moves ζ to 1.859 and removes
  iid equivalence. The basin interval excludes exact 2 but overlaps the margin,
  so the follow-up is inconclusive under its frozen rule. LAEA and geodesic
  AEQD agree; 379 invalid selected polygons, 31 unmatched outlets and an iid
  goodness-of-fit diagnostic (p=0.014) prevent a fully qualified positive claim.
- The separately frozen UK replication triggers its either-interval departure
  rule, but its spatial interval includes 2. It is not a robust spatial rejection.
- The seismic source/quality supplement fails the minimum-ten eligibility gate.
  Location errors reduce 16 sufficiently populated sequences to six with a usable
  distance span. The error proxy can be conservative; unknown covariance,
  magnitude-dependent memberships and nonuniform magnitude scales limit a future
  physical comparison. Neither prediction has been tested on this real cohort.
- The manuscript has been substantially developed on the merged branch; the
  earlier claim that it still has only September's framing is superseded.
- The Italian proxy route fails a necessary synthetic magnitude-estimator gate.
  This does not reject either b/D2 relation. A source-bin likelihood helps in
  noiseless single-source simulations but remains unvalidated for detection,
  measurement errors and shared calibration uncertainty. No automatic estimator
  replacement or real-data rerun is pending.
- Independent field quotas clear a conservative C/N stock prerequisite for
  nine nominal sample matches. Only three contain both cyanobacterial groups,
  so compatibility does not establish community closure or a binding resource.
  Pacific P remains unit-unqualified after numerical inspection. There is still
  no independently justified equal-opportunity measure for a natural-community
  allocation test; fitting it from the observed counts would be circular.
