# Where do fewer recipients receive about the same total?

23 November 2026. The [television example](tv-distribution-model.md) gives one
set to each person and divides the associated commissions and profits evenly
among five groups. The second assumption has a measurable consequence. If one
group has a quarter as many recipients as another, its mean additional payment
must be four times as large to give the groups equal totals.

## Gross commissions in a sales network

LifeVantage reports the annual dollars paid at each U.S. sales rank and the
monthly mean per paid consultant at that rank. Three adjacent ranks stood out
in the 2024 disclosure. Before opening the 2025 disclosure, we kept those same
three ranks, predicted one third of their combined commissions for each and
specified a comparison with equal pay per paid consultant-month. Both years
use monthly paid-as rank, so a consultant can contribute to different ranks
in different months. The amounts are gross of expenses. The 2025 table excludes
the recently acquired LoveBiome consultants and reports that 67.68% of U.S.
consultants with an opportunity to earn received zero commissions during the year.
[2024 U.S. disclosure, p. 2](https://www.lifevantage.com/legal/us-en/income-disclosure-statement-20250203.pdf) · [2025 U.S. disclosure, p. 2](https://cdn.lifevantage.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2026/02/27121010/260226.01-US-EN-Income-Disclosure-Statement.pdf)

| Managing Consultant rank | 2024 annual total | 2025 annual total | 2025 mean per paid month | Share of 2025 three-rank total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $6,760,908 | $6,555,441 | $1,707 | 33.93% |
| 2 | $6,548,280 | $6,207,939 | $3,383 | 32.13% |
| 3 | $7,225,417 | $6,556,330 | $6,801 | 33.94% |

Dividing the 2025 totals by the rounded monthly means gives approximately
3,840, 1,835 and 964 paid consultant-months. Rank 3 had about a quarter as
many as rank 1. Their annual pools differed by $889. The largest of the three
pools was 1.103 times the smallest in 2024 and 1.056 times in 2025.
Across **all 15 ranks**, pools ranged from $21,720 to $9,964,204 in 2024 and
from $6,100 to $9,634,929 in 2025.

Using half the sum of absolute share errors, the 2025 discrepancy from equal
rank totals was 0.012. Equal pay per consultant-month gives group shares of
57.84%, 27.64% and 14.52%, with discrepancy 0.239. This second calculation
uses counts inferred from the same table and serves as a descriptive benchmark.
The near-equality persisted within the selected part of one pay system. Each
rank carries equal weight in this comparison. Individual earnings vary within
ranks, so the rank table alone does not establish either log-orthopolic or
linear-orthopolic allocation over individual earnings.

## A rule that splits one sale

Young Living's U.S. plan effective April 2025 specifies sponsorship commissions
of 8%, 5%, 4%, 4% and 4% of eligible volume at successive depths. A qualifying
sale therefore allocates equal amounts at depths three through five when all
three recipients qualify. This is a contractual mechanism for one commission
component. To establish the realized totals across a network would require
sales volumes and qualification records by depth.
[U.S. compensation plan, pp. 2 and 6](https://static.youngliving.com/en-US/PDFS/April1_2025_YL_CompensationPlan_US_EN.pdf)

## Ocean biomass by body size

In Hatton and colleagues' reconstruction of the preindustrial upper ocean,
the estimated wet biomass in four consecutive body-mass classes was 0.891 Gt
(10–100 g), 0.874 Gt (100 g–1 kg), 0.844 Gt (1–10 kg) and 0.850 Gt (10–100 kg).
To reproduce the figures, sum the estimated biomass column over organism groups
at log-base-ten size midpoints 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 and 4.5 in the
[upper-ocean table](../data/raw/sheldon_summary_biomass_top200_table_long.csv).
Each tenfold interval carries the same weight, giving an approximately
**log-orthopolic** allocation of standing biomass over this range. Across all
23 intervals the largest total is 38.8 times the smallest. The end classes
dominated by bacteria and large whales depart most conspicuously from the
broad plateau. The measurement concerns the stock of biomass by individual
body size. Energy flow between trophic levels is a separate observable.
[Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8580314/) · [Released data](https://zenodo.org/records/5520055)

## A real public purchase of televisions

Mexico's federal audit recorded 10,112,260 digital televisions delivered from
May 2014 through March 2016 in a program for low-income households. A procurement
notice names five winning suppliers for one tender covering more than 2.65
million sets. Those records establish deliveries and identify suppliers. Testing
the five-group allocation requires the commissions, attributable profits and
recipient counts for each group on the same orders.
[Federal audit](https://www.asf.gob.mx/Trans/Informes/IR2016i/Documentos/Auditorias/2016_0321_a.pdf) · [Procurement award](https://www.gob.mx/sict/prensa/informa-sct-fallo-de-la-licitacion-para-adquisicion-de-televisores-digitales)
