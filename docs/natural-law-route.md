[orthopolity research programme](research-brief.md). Its observation gate

laboratory, field or hardware measurements are proposed.

**Theoretical continuation, 4 October:** the
[cost-intervention design](measure-intervention.md) establishes how to distinguish
two reference-measure rules that are identical under a power cost law. A physical
additive overhead makes their full-profile and abundance-response predictions
different. This is a conditional design with an analytic calculator; no eligible
natural-system application or intervention-stability rule has been established.

## The objective

Pursue a conditional empirical regularity with independently specified scope
and discriminating predictions. The current programme establishes useful
accounting, conditional forecasts and observation limits; it has not established
natural neutrality. A deeper mechanism is optional for an empirical law, but
observable scope, accurate predictions and independent replication are essential.

The next candidate statement is:

> In a prespecified population of pelagic mesozooplankton communities,
> expected chemically measured nitrogen stock per logarithmic sieve-size interval
> is approximately constant over a fixed, adequately observed domain.

Write R_j for nitrogen concentration in size class [a_j,b_j], and let E denote
the declared sampling population or independently observed environmental regime.
The candidate predicts

$$E[R_j\mid E]=C(E)\ln(b_j/a_j).$$

C(E) is an unknown common amplitude. It is not a measured available budget and
does not establish nitrogen limitation or exhaustion. A test of this stock
pattern requires direct stocks and a defensible observation model; individual
costs and counts become additional requirements for a cost-based abundance
prediction, and independently measured budgets for a resource-feasibility explanation.
Nitrogen is selected for direct assay quality, not because its biological
priority or limiting role has already been demonstrated.

Population-average equality is weaker than neutrality in every community.
Environmental mixtures can obscure departures, so the conditioning, sampling
weights and intended generalization must be specified before evaluation. No
validated biological admission rule for a neutral regime has yet been identified.
The exact regime and tolerance therefore remain open protocol decisions.

## New predictive content

Equal material across size classes has substantial precedent in
[Sheldon et al. (1972)](https://doi.org/10.4319/lo.1972.17.3.0327), and
[Cuesta, Delius and Law](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.04158) supply an explicit
size-spectrum model. Independently measured cost-to-capacity prediction also
has close precedent in [Marshall et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2200713119).
An accounting identity or a new name for equal biomass cannot supply a new law.

The chemical-stock comparison can first establish whether one additional
measured resource follows a restricted regularity. To advance the broader
orthopolity claim, add a prediction fixed before evaluation outcomes: an independently
observable regime boundary, a quantified response to a documented change, or
resource-specific allocations predicted from separately measured traits.
Choosing nitrogen instead of dry mass supplies an empirical extension; it does
not alone establish a distinct general theory. A successful two-cruise study
would warrant a restricted replicated result, not universality across nature.

## Candidate archives and their observation gate

### MALASPINA, PANGAEA 816451

The retained [source audit](next-dataset-audit.md) and
[archive metadata](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.816451) identify
chemically assayed nitrogen stocks in common-net fractions 200–500,
500–1000 and 1000–2000 µm. Exclude the separate-net 40–200 µm sample and
the open upper fraction. Over this bounded domain, logarithmic allocation
predicts integrated stock proportions (0.39794, 0.30103, 0.30103), while
linear allocation predicts (0.16667, 0.27778, 0.55556).

The [actual Leg 8 methods](https://doi.org/10.1093/plankt/fbt011) describe
a January–March 2011 transect, mostly near 24°N, with daytime vertical catches
through the upper 200 m. They confirm unacidified elemental assays and the
open >2000 µm fraction; the earlier audit's exact pretreatment uncertainty
is now resolved. Nitrogen remains the preferred endpoint. Isotope precision
is not elemental-stock precision.

No complete usable tow count, biological repeat-haul allocation or independent
sampling-unit count has been established from the reviewed metadata. The
archive's 1,161 data points do not represent 1,161 ecological replicates.
Relevant observation information includes filtered volumes, class aliquot/split
factors, fraction recovery and relative capture, blanks, detection limits and
nitrogen-assay repeatability. Not every raw QC record is mandatory: traceable
normalization and a justified error model or bounds may suffice. The completed
gate establishes 43 station events, without identifying independent complete
assays. Event/location/time fields alone cannot resolve relative class bias.
Shared volume error may cancel within one
correctly scaled catch share, but this does not solve class-specific errors
or identify the ratio of expected water-column stocks.

### Potential independent replication: BLOOFINZ-IO

[BCO-DMO 956590, version 1](https://www.bco-dmo.org/dataset/956590/Dataset_description.pdf)
describes size-fractionated mesozooplankton biomass and elemental assays from
an independent 2022 Indian Ocean cruise. Its 202 µm net collections include
0.2–0.5, 0.5–1 and 1–2 mm sieve classes. This is a promising same-resource,
same-domain replication lead; matching class labels does not establish matching
sampling. Audit gear, tow/depth/time protocols, sample splitting, filters,
rinsing, concentration normalization and independent sampling blocks before
declaring comparability. It is not yet an eligible replication dataset.

PELACUS dry mass cannot independently replicate a nitrogen law. A dry-mass
law using both sources would require a separate declared hypothesis; results
must not motivate switching resources after evaluation.

## The finite next study

1. **One methods-only gate for these two archives.** Establish sample identities,
   observation corrections, uncertainty information and independent replication.
   Seek publicly linked tow/sample/QC records; do not request new measurements.
   If the ecological target is not identifiable, stop this law-test route and
   retain a descriptive observation audit. A narrower operational-catch study
   would need a separate protocol. Do not compensate with
   more catalogues of the same inadequate design.
2. **Specify the claim and admissible population.** Use geography, season,
   depth, protocol and independently measured environment fields. Eligibility
   cannot use flatness, fitted slopes, successful model scores or retrospectively
   declared equilibrium. Declare the exact stock target, support, tails,
   weighting, missingness, practically meaningful margin and replication unit.
3. **Calibrate the actual observation design.** Simulate plausible capture,
   assay error, censoring and haul/station dependence. Demonstrate reliable
   uncertainty and useful discrimination at the chosen margin before evaluation outcomes.
   Do not transfer the plant iid model or previous 48-block gate unchanged.
   If precision or power is inadequate, retain an underpowered/unresolved design.
4. **Freeze competitors and evaluation.** Compare logarithmic and linear
   allocation with development-only empirical stocks and a fitted stock-density
   tilt. Three classes give only two independent profile contrasts. Missing
   class counts do not support count-based Pareto/Weibull fits. Assign complete
   cruises/regions to development and evaluation using metadata; reserve the
   second source's outcomes for replication. The allocation weights are fixed,
   while comparator fitting and observation calibration use development only.
5. **Estimate expected raw stocks first.** Paired contrasts such as
   R_j/ln(b_j/a_j) − R_1/ln(b_1/a_1) target equal expected stock density.
   Average complete sampling blocks under the declared weights; show normalized
   expected means afterward. The mean of normalized individual catches targets
   a different quantity. Biological repeats and cruises, not sieve classes or
   analytical aliquots, determine ecological replication.
6. **Retain outcomes that can change the claim.** A calibrated practical
   equivalence result and successful transfer would support the stated scope.
   A robust departure in an eligible evaluation region contradicts that scope.
   An unresolved result supplies neither a pass nor a failure. Do not rescue
   it by changing resource, domain, habitat exclusions, corrections or margins.
   A revised hypothesis receives a new protocol and independent evaluation.
