# Manuscript and evidence review

10 November 2026. The general-law title and research ambition are retained.
The strongest current contribution is the distinction between exact accounting,
specified allocation mechanisms, transferred empirical predictions and failures.
The main article already states that the natural populations examined do not
establish a common neutral regime. This review checks that interpretation
against the supplement and selected retained numerical artifacts; it does not
add observations, rerun empirical fits or independently revalidate the entire
bibliography.

**11 November navigation update:** section links below follow the clearer
main-article order. The [conceptual revision review](manuscript-revision-review-2026-11-11.md)

## Claim-to-evidence map

“Derived” means a consequence of stated mathematical or physical premises.
“Constructed” means a specified model or generated simulation, not observations.
“Empirical transfer” means measured inputs and distinct evaluated outcomes;
the local freeze does not erase the prior access disclosed in each report.
Most public-data studies are retrospective, and none is externally preregistered.
These categories are not interchangeable evidence for the law's general reach.

## Findings and corrections

1. **The runtime comparator was mislabeled in the summaries.** Main 4.2 and
   S5.1 called the 0.126–0.273 comparison a “unit-cost alternative.” The
   [frozen study report](dimensionality-intervention.md#frozen-allocation-and-restriction-predictions)
   instead specifies cost degree one, q proportional to matrix side length.
   The retained JSON key `unit_cost_fair_cpu` is a historical identifier, not
   a reason to describe q as constant. The manuscript summaries now call it
   a linear-cost alternative and give the formula; results are unchanged.
2. **The Dunaliella summary overstated its ranking.** S5.3's short table said
   the equal-biovolume forecast was best. In the retained biovolume evaluation,
   fold B's assigned-carbon MAE is 0.175485, compared with 0.179205 for equal
   biovolume. The table now states the supported comparison: equal biovolume
   beats the fitted size law and equal counts in both folds. Main 4.3 already
   made that narrower claim. The adjacent raw-OD description now restricts
   its unchanged-ordering statement to the first four comparisons: fold B's
   equal-biovolume/assigned-carbon point ranking reverses between biovolume
   and OD, while their frozen comparison remains not distinguished. The full
   comparison table and frozen outputs remain unchanged.
3. **The supplement's synthesis lagged behind later studies.** The S5 opening
   now includes chemical, physical and language transfers. S5.8 distinguishes
   actual runtime allocation from separate-culture closure and summarizes later
   successes and failures with their measures and stronger comparators. It no
   longer describes all positive results as budget closure.
4. **The registry guide lacked its latest entry.** Its catalogue now leads with
   the retained thermal transfer and the verified total of 46 records / 1,168
   references, preserving all historical paragraphs. Later assembly and interface
   work added no empirical records. Registry size is not a confirmation count.
5. **Three figure captions detached during PDF rendering.** S9 combined its
   italic caption with a subsequent ordinary sentence; S14 and S15 used bold
   labels rather than the converter's standalone italic-caption convention.
   The source formatting now groups each caption with its image, preserving
   every caption's scientific text.

These are corrections to labels, comparative claims and synthesis. They do
not change a hypothesis, acceptance threshold, observation, forecast or verdict.
The headline numerical checks above otherwise agree with the retained artifacts
at the manuscript's rounding precision. This is a targeted consistency check,
not a claim that every number or proof has received a new independent audit.

## Remaining priorities for submission

- **Resolve declarations and article packaging with the authors.** Main's
  opening notice still leaves authorship and submission declarations unsettled.
  Contributions, funding, acknowledgements and any venue-required assistance
  disclosure need actual author information. The private 2017 manuscript's
  declarations cannot simply be copied.
- **Finish source-specific permissions and attribution work.**
  [The data notice](../data/NOTICE.md) still records conflicting GLOSSAQUA
  licence statements, unverified Hatton table terms and no explicit licence
  for the pinned plant-data repository. Public accessibility and the package
  licence do not resolve those recorded questions. This review did not refresh
  external licences or change redistribution status.
- **Choose a venue before final formatting.** Preserve the general-law
  programme while describing the contribution as exact conditional content,
  mechanisms and scoped transfers, with broad natural applicability still
  under test. Current venue scope, novelty requirements, declarations and
  reference rules require a separate current-source check; no venue selection
  is implied by this local review.
- **Keep unresolved empirical eligibility visible.** A stronger natural
  allocation claim requires independently fixed costs, reference measure,
  constraints and a distinct prediction that passes. The
  [storage intervention](storage-intervention-design-2026-10-25.md) remains a
  concrete scientific priority, but the audited archives do not supply its
  closed intervention. Another fitted exponent or reinterpretation of exposed
  outcomes does not fill that gap. The bounded source-search stopping rules
  remain in force absent new qualifying information.
