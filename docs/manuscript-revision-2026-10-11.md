## Corrections to biological interpretation

The size-selected Dunaliella comparison is between separately grown cultures.
Its large/small geometric mean capacity ratio is 0.97482567, across a
10.4478654-fold difference in mean cell volume. The near equality concerns
those two extreme treatments. From the retained mean log capacities,
11.88927483 for controls, 11.72292953 for small and 11.69743290 for large,
control geometric mean capacity is approximately **18–21% higher**. Equality
across all treatments or individual lineages does not follow. The earlier
19–21% approximation used rounded log means; the revised text uses the
unrounded retained values. The original numerical outputs remain unchanged.

Phosphorus-deprived culture recovery is approximately +0.25 to +0.31 **log
units**, not a 25–31% increase. These correspond to about 28–36% on the original
scale. The supporting comparisons use log units to match the retained results.

The original chemostat ranges are per-vessel **maximum attainable** versus
**maximum observed** redistribution over days 0–12, not matched-time forecasts.
The ordinal endpoint concerns the smallest time-weighted log share growth;
4/12 alone is not labelled a chance outcome without a null model. Recovery
means the declared return within 0.1 total variation at two consecutive
observations. The revised summaries use these operational definitions.

## Additions and their evidential status

The [gravity derivation](gravity-realization.md) distinguishes complete source
accounting, angular field-flux equality and disjoint shell energy. It is an
interpretation of established Newtonian equations, with explicit geometry and
quantity; it is not a new gravitational observation or a derivation of Newton's
law from resource allocation.

The [chemostat follow-up protocol](chemostat-capacity-followup-protocol-2026-10-10.md)
tests whether a larger class of carbon/nitrogen price changes can accommodate
previously observed redistribution. It is a retrospective conditional diagnosis,
not a new independent validation. Its recorded outcomes and qualifications belong
alongside the original failed forecast, not in its place.

The central general-law claim remains. Exact results establish their stated
domains; transferred predictions assess additional physical reach. Explaining
a departure requires independent evidence and consequences beyond a fitted
correction. That distinction keeps the ambition scientifically testable.
